CONTENT CREATOR AGREEMENT: Winter Radiance

Parties: Northpeak Media ("Agency"), acting for its client Aurelia Cosmetics ("Brand"), and the undersigned creator ("Creator").

1. Content. Creator will produce 1 TikTok video and 1 Instagram Reel, both posted by 2026-12-15.
2. Compensation. The total fee is $2,800. Agency pays Creator once Agency has itself been paid by Aurelia Cosmetics for the campaign.
3. Ownership. All Content becomes the exclusive property of Brand upon creation, and Brand may use it for as long as Brand determines, on any platform.
4. Make-goods. If a post receives fewer views than Brand expected, Creator will return all compensation received or produce additional content at no cost.
5. Approvals. Creator agrees that any feedback from Brand will be implemented, with no limit on the number of rounds.
