INFLUENCER AGREEMENT: Glow Up Campaign

This agreement is between Brightway Agency, on behalf of its client Solace Skin ("Brand"), and the creator ("Creator").

1. Deliverables. Creator will publish 2 Instagram Reels by 2026-12-12.
2. Fee. Brand will pay Creator a total fee of $3,000.
3. Payment. Agency will pay Creator within 15 days after receipt of payment from the Client. Payment terms: Net 90.
4. Usage. Creator grants Brand an irrevocable, worldwide license to use the Content in perpetuity, in any and all media now known or hereafter devised, including paid advertising.
5. Revisions. Creator will make unlimited revisions until Brand is fully satisfied.
6. Termination. Brand may terminate this Agreement at any time for any reason.
7. Exclusivity. Creator will not work with any competing brands in the skincare category for 12 months.
