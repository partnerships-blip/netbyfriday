CREATOR PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT: Marigold Holiday Glow

This Creator Partnership Agreement is made between Marigold Beauty Co. ("Brand") and the content creator named in the signature block ("Creator"). It is effective as of the date both parties sign, 2026-11-03.

1. Fee. Brand will pay Creator a total fee of $6,500.00 for the deliverables below.

2. Payment. 50% of the fee is due within 15 days of signing. The balance is due Net 30 after the final deliverable goes live. Creator will invoice Brand for each payment.

3. Deliverables. Creator will create and publish:
   (a) one (1) Instagram Reel, live by 2026-11-20;
   (b) three (3) Instagram Stories with link sticker, live by 2026-11-21;
   (c) one (1) TikTok video, live by 2026-11-25.
   Brand will review drafts within 3 business days. Creator will provide up to two rounds of revisions per deliverable.

4. Usage rights. Brand may use the deliverables in paid social advertising on Meta (Instagram, Facebook) and TikTok for 90 days from the first post date. Organic reposting on Brand's own channels is allowed during the same period. Any use after that period requires a new agreement and fee.

5. Whitelisting. Brand may run the deliverables as partnership ads or Spark Ads from Creator's handle during the usage period, with ad copy approved by Creator in advance.

6. Exclusivity. Creator will not enter paid partnerships with competing color cosmetics brands for 30 days after the final deliverable goes live.

7. Cancellation. If Brand cancels after signing for reasons other than Creator's breach, Brand will pay 50% of the fee as a kill fee.

8. Disclosure. Creator will clearly disclose the partnership in every post (for example, #ad or the platform's paid partnership label).

Signed by both parties on 2026-11-03.
